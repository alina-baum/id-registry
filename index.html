Scope*
US FDA-approved products currently marketed, plus development-stage, discontinued, EUA-only and Phase 3 failure entries — see the Status column. Some entries are deliberately not FDA-approved. Products with major regulatory standing elsewhere are included where omitting them would misrepresent the field: Hibsago (Japan), Ebvallo (EU), and the malaria vaccines Mosquirix and R21/Matrix-M (WHO-recommended and prequalified). These carry "Approved elsewhere (not FDA)" or an equivalent note in Status.
What is included
The approved sections aim to be complete. The pipeline sections are curated, not exhaustive. The working floor is Phase 2 and above, with Phase 1 assets admitted by exception where there is a strong signal — a major acquisition, a pivotal designation, or a pathogen with no other clinical-stage asset here. Phase 3 failures are included because a definitive trial carries information; Phase 2 failures generally are not, since most assets entering Phase 2 do not proceed and listing them all would add noise rather than signal. Selection reflects judgement about what is informative, weighted toward novel mechanisms, pathogens with no approved options, and programmes whose trajectory says something. Gaps certainly remain.

Approval year
Original US FDA approval. Blank where the product was never FDA-approved.
Indication
Prevention covers all preventive use — products labelled for prevention, prophylaxis or pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) — since these reflect terminology differences between fields rather than different purposes. Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) is kept separate because it follows a specific known exposure. Exact label language is in the notes.
Next milestone
Expected readout, regulatory date or trial step where publicly disclosed. Sponsor-estimated and subject to change; blank where nothing is public.
2025 Sales
Full-year worldwide sales of the product, in the currency the company reports (£ GSK/ViiV, € Sanofi). Sterling and euro figures carry a USD equivalent converted at 2025 annual average rates (GBP 1.32, EUR 1.13); the as-reported figure comes first. n/d = not disclosed at product level; n/a = not applicable.
Partnered products
The figure shown is total product sales, not one partner's revenue share. Where a company reports several products on one line, the combined figure appears on each row with its composition named — those rows should be counted once, not summed. Examples: Prezista/Prezcobix/Rezolsta/Symtuza (J&J), ProQuad/M-M-R II/Varivax (Merck), ‡ Gilead "Other HIV" ($500M total), § Gilead "Other Liver Disease" ($874M total), ¶ Gardasil + Gardasil 9, * combined franchise (Moderna COVID; Sanofi influenza), "(franchise)" (Sanofi Polio/Pertussis/Hib; GSK meningitis; Pfizer Prevnar). Where partners account separately and no clean product total exists — Comirnaty — the reported figure is used and the limitation noted in that entry.
Other conventions
Combination products appear under each relevant pathogen. Bacterial agents are grouped by pathogen where pathogen-directed, and by drug class where broad-spectrum.
